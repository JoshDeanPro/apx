# APX Contributor License Agreement — DRAFT

> **Status: draft, not yet in force.** This document describes the
> *intended* contribution model. It has not been reviewed by an
> attorney and there is currently no acceptance mechanism (no
> click-through, no CLA-bot, no signed copies on file). **Nothing in
> this file is currently a binding legal agreement.** Treat it as a
> statement of intent for how contributions are meant to work, pending
> proper legal review. This distinction matters and is deliberate — see
> *is* actually in force today (the MPL-2.0 license itself).

## The model, once finalized

**Contributor License Agreement, not copyright assignment.** These are
different things, and APX intends the lighter one:

- **CLA (intended model):** you keep ownership of your contribution. You
  grant the official APX Project sufficiently broad rights to use,
  modify, distribute, sublicense, maintain, incorporate, and release your
  accepted contribution as part of APX — including rights that support
  the project's ability to maintain APX over the long term.
- **Copyright assignment (not what's intended here):** ownership itself
  transfers to the project. APX does not require this today. If the
  Steward later determines single-owner copyright over the canonical
  implementation is actually necessary, that decision should be made
  deliberately, designed with an IP/open-source attorney, and never
  applied retroactively or silently to contributions already made under
  a lighter CLA.

## What this is not

- Not an assertion that the project can unilaterally relicense your
  contribution under different terms than what you agreed to. Any future
  relicensing right would have to be granted explicitly by a properly
  reviewed and accepted CLA — it is not implied by anything in this
  draft.
- Not a claim that contributing today already binds you to a CLA. Until
  a real acceptance mechanism exists, contributions are governed by
  [LICENSE](LICENSE) (MPL-2.0) and the license grant described in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — nothing more.

## Action required for the project owner

Before this becomes a real, binding CLA:

1. Have the actual agreement text drafted or reviewed by an open-source
   or IP attorney.
2. Stand up a real acceptance mechanism (a CLA bot on PRs, a signed
   document flow, or equivalent) so "agreed" means something verifiable.
3. Update this file to remove the draft status once both of the above
   are true, and only then start requiring it for new contributions.

Until then, this file documents intent, not obligation.
