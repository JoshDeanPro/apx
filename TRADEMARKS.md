# APX / OpenPower Trademark Policy

This policy covers the **names and logos** "APX" and "OpenPower" and
related official designations. It is separate from [LICENSE](LICENSE),
which covers the source code. You can be fully compliant with the MPL-2.0
license and still misuse the trademarks, and vice versa — read both.

## Status of these marks

"APX" and "OpenPower" are used here as claimed marks (APX™, OpenPower™).
**Neither is currently a registered trademark.** Owning the domains
openpower.one or openpower.dev does not, by itself, establish trademark
registration, and this document does not claim otherwise. See
[Action required](#action-required-for-the-project-owner) below.

## What's always fine, no permission needed

Truthful, descriptive use that doesn't imply you *are* the official
project:

- "Compatible with APX"
- "Built using APX"
- "APX integration"
- "Forked from APX"
- "Powered by APX"
- Referring to APX or OpenPower by name in documentation, articles,
  comparisons, or discussion

## What needs authorization

- Calling your distribution or fork "Official APX" or "Official
  OpenPower"
- Using the APX or OpenPower logos in a way that implies endorsement or
  official status you don't have
- Naming a competing distribution in a way likely to confuse people about
  whether it's the canonical project
- Misrepresenting a fork as the canonical APX project or an official
  release

## Official designations

The canonical APX Project (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) controls
the meaning of, and who may use, these specific designations:

- **Official APX**
- **Official APX SDK**
- **Official APX Provider**
- **APX Compatible**
- **APX Commerce Compatible**
- **Official OpenPower Integration**

These are not in active use yet — this section establishes the
structural foundation for them (see [CONFORMANCE.md](CONFORMANCE.md)),
not a live certification program.

## Forks

Forks are fully permitted under [LICENSE](LICENSE). If your fork
materially diverges from upstream APX, please pick a clearly
differentiated name — this isn't a restriction on forking, it's the same
courtesy that keeps any open ecosystem legible. You may truthfully say
your fork is "based on APX" or "forked from APX."

## The intent here

This policy exists to prevent confusion and impersonation, not to
suppress the ecosystem. If you're building something real with APX and
you're not sure whether a particular usage needs authorization, the
answer is almost always "describe what you actually did, don't claim to
be us" — and that's fine.

## Action required for the project owner

This document is not a substitute for legal advice. Before relying on
these marks commercially at scale, the project owner should have
professional trademark clearance and registration considered for:

- "APX"
- "OpenPower"
- any associated logos, once designed
- specific compatibility marks, once those programs are real

No application has been filed as part of this document; none is filed
automatically by anything in this repository.
